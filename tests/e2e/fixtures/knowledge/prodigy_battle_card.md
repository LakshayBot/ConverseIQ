# Prodigy - Three-Phase CT-Operated Smart Meter

Prodigy is Secure Meters' flagship three-phase CT-operated smart meter for
urban distribution networks and bulk consumer points.

## Key differentiator: built-in current transformers

Unlike conventional three-phase meters that require external CTs, Prodigy's
current transformers are **built in** - thread-through connections mean the
field team simply passes the cable directly through the meter. There are no
external CT kits or accessories to transport, install, or maintain. This
eliminates the installation pain that field teams experience with external
CT-operated units, where separate CT hardware must be lugged to site and
wired by hand.

## Installation and field operations

- Thread-through connections: no external CTs, no accessories, no separate
  CT kits to carry to site.
- Field teams install the meter in a single pass; a Delhi discom client
  reduced installation time by forty percent after switching to Prodigy.
- Fewer installation errors: the built-in CT wiring cannot be miswired on
  site, which protects metering accuracy over the meter's service life.

## Accuracy over the deployment life

External CT installations are a known failure point in three-phase metering:
connection resistance and ageing CT hardware cause metering accuracy to
drift after about six months in the field, forcing periodic re-commissioning
of the external CT wiring. Because Prodigy's current transformers are built
in and factory-calibrated, the accuracy class is stable across the
deployment - no field-team CT maintenance, no accuracy re-verification
visits, no accuracy drift complaints from urban rollout programs.

## Product summary

- Three-phase, CT-operated with built-in thread-through current transformers
- Class 0.2S accuracy for bulk consumer points (Apex 100 covers
  transmission-level precision needs)
- Designed for urban rollout programs replacing legacy external-CT metering
- Proven installation-time savings versus external CT installations
