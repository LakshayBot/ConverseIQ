# Objection Handling Guide

## Pricing Objections

### "It's too expensive"

**Response Strategy:**
- Acknowledge the concern with empathy
- Highlight ROI: customers typically see 30% improvement in close rates
- Offer flexible plans: monthly, annual, and enterprise
- Reference case studies where customers saved money by switching

**Supporting Data:**
- Average sales rep spends 2 hours/day searching for information → CallPilot AI saves 1.5 hours/day
- At $50/hour fully loaded cost, that's $75/day savings per rep
- For a 5-person team: $75 × 5 × 250 days = $93,750/year in recovered productivity

### "We don't have budget this quarter"

**Response Strategy:**
- Offer a pilot program at reduced cost
- Suggest starting with a small team (1-2 reps)
- Remind them budget cycles are flexible for ROI-positive tools
- Offer to help build the internal business case

## Security Objections

### "We have concerns about data privacy"

**Response Strategy:**
- CallPilot AI is SOC 2 Type II compliant
- All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- BYOK support means you own and control your AI keys
- Self-hosted option for complete data sovereignty
- No customer audio is stored - only transcript text

### "Our security team needs to review"

**Response Strategy:**
- Offer a security review call with our engineering team
- Share our SOC 2 report and penetration test results
- Provide architecture documentation for review
- Offer a sandbox environment for security testing

## Integration Objections

### "Does it work with our existing tools?"

**Response Strategy:**
- CallPilot AI is platform-agnostic (works with Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack)
- Desktop Agent captures any desktop audio - no browser extensions needed
- Phase 2 includes CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- REST API for custom integrations

## Implementation Objections

### "We don't have time to implement"

**Response Strategy:**
- Setup takes under 1 hour (Docker Compose or cloud)
- Desktop Agent installs in under 2 minutes
- No training needed - the AI works silently alongside sales reps
- We offer white-glove onboarding for Enterprise customers

### "Our team won't use it"

**Response Strategy:**
- The AI never interrupts - salespeople don't need to change their workflow
- It's like having a silent sales engineer in every meeting
- Early adopter teams report 40% reduction in post-meeting admin work
- Offer a 14-day free trial with no commitment
